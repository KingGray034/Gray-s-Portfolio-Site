// Who I am

ABOUT.

I'm a Full Stack Engineer based in Abuja, Nigeria. My job is building complete systems — the frontend, backend, database, and everything connecting them.

My main stack is Next.js, TypeScript, tRPC, Prisma, and Supabase. My most recent project is Billet, a job tracking tool with AI-powered resume analysis and Gmail integration. I also work on NudgePal, a task assistant designed for neurodivergent users. I'm drawn to projects where the frontend and backend need to be designed together from the start — that's where most of the interesting problems live.
